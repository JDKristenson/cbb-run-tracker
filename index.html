How It Works

1

Monitor Every Game

We track all D1 men's college basketball games in real-time, monitoring score changes every 15 seconds.

2

Detect Underdog Runs

When a betting underdog goes on an 8-0 or larger scoring run, that's our trigger. These runs often cause market overreaction.

3

Alert with Context

You get the score, time, ESPN's live win probability, and the closing line favorite. All the data you need to make a decision.

The Strategy

When underdogs go on runs, casual bettors often pile on, moving the live line. But favorites are favorites for a reason. They usually recover. By betting the closing line favorite (the pre-game favorite), you're betting on regression to the mean with better odds than the live line offers.
